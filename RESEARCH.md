# Brand.Life — Concept & Competitive Research

## 1. The winning idea

**Brand.Life = a free brand-building toolkit + education hub, monetized by AdSense, lead generation and sponsorship.**

The domain reads as "the life of a brand", so the site is organized around an original framework — the **Brand Life Cycle** (Spark → Launch → Grow → Scale → Renew) — and every tool, guide and call to action maps to a stage.

### Why this beats the alternatives for this domain

| Option considered | Traffic potential | Ad value (CPC) | Lead value | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lifestyle/"brand life" blog | Medium | Low | Low | Rejected — vague, low CPC |
| Brand news magazine | Medium | Medium | Low | Kept only as a self-updating news module |
| Logo maker SaaS | High | High | Medium | Too expensive to build/host on free GitHub Pages |
| **Free tools + guides + audit/lead marketplace** | **High (tool + how-to search demand, evergreen)** | **High (branding, logo design, marketing software, business formation, domains)** | **High (branding projects are $500–$50k+)** | **Chosen** |

### Revenue stack (in order of expected impact)

1. **Lead generation** — Free Brand Audit (3-step form) + "Get matched with experts" form. Branding/design project leads can be referred or sold to agencies/freelancers; the expert-network form builds the supply side.
2. **Google AdSense** — Auto ads on 41 pages; tool pages generate repeat sessions and long dwell time; guides target commercial-intent queries (naming, logo, rebrand, brand strategy).
3. **Sponsorship / advertising** — newsletter, tool sponsor, contest partner, sponsored guide, expert-network listing.
4. **Affiliate-ready links** — "Check .com" buttons point to a registrar search (swap in an affiliate URL later).
5. **Reader support** — pledge tiers now; drop PayPal / Stripe / Buy Me a Coffee links into `assets/js/config.js` to switch on instant checkout.
6. **Contests** — engagement + sponsor-funded prize pools.

## 3. Feature checklist derived from the research

- [x] 10 free in-browser tools (no account, no storage)
- [x] Score-first lead magnet with gated personalized plan
- [x] 3-step lead form with progress bar, budget and timeline qualifiers
- [x] Two-sided marketplace forms (clients + experts)
- [x] Pillar guides with TOC, inline tool CTAs and mid-article lead box
- [x] Original framework (Brand Life Cycle) as the brand's signature idea
- [x] Self-updating news (daily GitHub Action) + daily rotating tip
- [x] Lite YouTube embeds (privacy-friendly, fast)
- [x] Contests with official rules and sponsor intake
- [x] Donation tiers with pledge fallback and payment-link switch
- [x] Hiring page with application form
- [x] Advertising/media-kit page
- [x] Full AdSense compliance set: privacy (with Google cookie language), terms, disclaimer, about, contact, ads.txt, cookie choice, sitemap, robots
- [x] Trademark/copyright disclosure and takedown process
- [x] Dark mode, responsive, accessible (skip link, labels, contrast)
