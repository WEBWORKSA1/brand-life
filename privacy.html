Privacy Policy

Last updated November 06, 2026

This policy explains what information Brand.Life ("we", "us") collects when you use brand.life, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit a form — such as a brand audit request, newsletter signup, contest entry, application or message — we receive the details you enter (for example your name, email address, website and message). Form submissions are delivered to us by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-processing service. We use these details only to respond to you, provide what you asked for, run contests, and, if you asked for an introduction, share the relevant project details with the expert(s) you agreed to be introduced to. We do not sell your personal information.

Tools

Our tools run in your browser. What you type into a tool is not sent to us unless you choose to submit an accompanying form. Some preferences (such as dark mode, saved name ideas and your cookie choice) are stored in your browser's local storage on your device only.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

When you first visit, our cookie notice lets you choose "Accept all" or "Essential only". If you choose essential only, we request non-personalized ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland may also see a consent message provided through Google's certified consent tools.

Other third parties

  • Google Fonts delivers our typefaces; your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.
  • YouTube videos load from youtube-nocookie.com only after you click play.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and may log basic technical information such as IP addresses for security.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or PIPEDA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, use our contact form with the topic "Privacy request".

Retention and security

We keep form submissions only as long as needed for the purpose you submitted them for, or as required by law, and take reasonable measures to protect them.

Children

Brand.Life is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). Contests are open only to people aged 18 or over.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
