FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Brand.Life really free?

Yes. Every tool, quiz and guide is free and works in your browser without an account. The site is supported by advertising, sponsors and voluntary support from readers.

Do you store what I type into the tools?

No. The tools run entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to submit a form, such as the free brand audit.

What is the free brand audit?

You share your website or social profile and what you need. A branding specialist reviews it and replies with your three biggest opportunities. There is no obligation to buy anything.

Can I use names generated here?

Generated names are suggestions. Before using one, check domain availability and run a trademark search in your market — our naming guide explains how. We don't claim any rights over names you create.

Is this site connected to any company called BrandLife?

No. Brand.Life is an independent educational website and is not affiliated with any company that uses a similar name. See our trademark disclosure.
