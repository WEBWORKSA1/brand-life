About

A brand is alive. We help it thrive.

Brand.Life is an independent, free resource for anyone building a brand: first-time founders, freelancers, creators, nonprofits and small marketing teams. We believe good branding shouldn't require an agency budget — it requires clear decisions, a few good tools and the discipline to be consistent.

What we do

  • Free tools that run in your browser and never store what you type.
  • Practical guides built around the Brand Life Cycle — Spark, Launch, Grow, Scale and Renew.
  • Free brand audits and introductions to vetted professionals when you need hands-on help.
  • Monthly challenges that give emerging creative talent a stage and a paycheck.

Our editorial standards

Every guide is written for usefulness first. We cite primary sources where we use data, avoid unverifiable claims, label sponsored content clearly and update articles when things change. Advertising and partnerships never influence our recommendations. Spot something wrong? Tell us and we'll fix it.

How we're funded

Brand.Life is supported by display advertising (Google AdSense), clearly labeled sponsorships, optional reader support and introductions to experts in our network. The tools and guides are free and always will be.

Independence

Brand.Life is not affiliated with any company that uses "BrandLife", "Brand Life" or similar names. See our trademark & copyright disclosure.
