FAQ
What should a brand style guide include?

At minimum: mission, audience, logo rules, color values, typography, and voice. That's enough to keep anyone who creates content on-brand.

How do I save it?

Click Print or save as PDF and choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog. Only the guide is printed.
