    FAQ
    What does the Brand Health Score measure?

    Twenty statements across five dimensions: clarity, identity, consistency, visibility and loyalty. Each answer is scored 0–4 and converted to a score out of 100.

    What's a good score?

    80+ is thriving, 60–79 growing, 40–59 fragile and under 40 at risk. The most useful output is your weakest dimension — that's where effort pays back fastest.

    Do you store my answers?

    Only if you request the emailed plan; then your scores are sent with your request so we can personalize it.
