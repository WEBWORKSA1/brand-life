FAQ
What are brand archetypes?

Twelve recurring character patterns, drawn from Carl Jung's work and popularized for marketing by Margaret Mark and Carol Pearson. They help keep a brand's personality consistent.

Is the result scientific?

No — it's a creative alignment tool, not a psychometric test. Use it to start a conversation about who your brand is.
