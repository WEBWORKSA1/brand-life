FAQ
How many colors should a brand have?
Usually one primary, one or two secondary and a neutral range. A rough 60-30-10 split between neutral, secondary and primary keeps things balanced.
What contrast ratio do I need?
WCAG AA asks for at least 4.5:1 for normal body text and 3:1 for large text. The table shows which of your colors pass on white or black.
