FAQ
Why only two fonts?
Two typefaces — one for headlines, one for body — give enough contrast for hierarchy while keeping the brand consistent. A third font is rarely necessary.
Can I use Google Fonts commercially?
Fonts in the Google Fonts library are published under open licenses such as the SIL Open Font License, which permit commercial use. Check each family's license page to be sure.
