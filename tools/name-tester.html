    FAQ
    What makes a good brand name score?

    Names score highest when they're 4–9 letters, three syllables or fewer, spelled the way they sound, have a natural vowel balance and open on a strong consonant.

    Is a low score a dealbreaker?

    No. Some famous brands would score modestly. The score flags risks so you can decide deliberately — for example, a hard-to-spell name needs more marketing to become searchable.

    Does this check trademarks?

    No. It links to official databases (USPTO and WIPO) so you can search. For anything you'll invest in, ask a trademark attorney for a full clearance search.
