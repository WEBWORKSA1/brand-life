FAQ
First or third person?

First person feels personal on social and your own site. Third person suits speaker introductions, press and company team pages.

How long should a bio be?

Social bios are usually limited to about 150–160 characters. A short bio is 1–2 sentences; a medium bio about 60–100 words.
