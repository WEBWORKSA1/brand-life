FAQ
How does the brand name generator work?

It combines your keywords with industry word banks, suffixes, prefixes and invented syllables, then scores every result with the same rules as our Name Tester. Click Generate again for a fresh batch.

Are the names available?

We can't guarantee availability. Use the Check .com link for each name, then run a trademark search before you commit.

Can I use a name I find here?

Yes — we claim no rights over generated names. Make sure nobody else has rights to it in your market first.
