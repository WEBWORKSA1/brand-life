FAQ
What makes a strong tagline?

Short (under eight words), specific to you, and focused on what the customer gets. Read it aloud next to a competitor's name — if it still fits, keep editing.

Tagline vs. slogan?

A tagline is a long-lived line attached to your brand identity. A slogan usually belongs to a single campaign. This tool helps with both.
